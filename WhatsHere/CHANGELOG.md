# What's Here

## 0.2.1
- First release on CurseForge. Same features as 0.2.0.

## 0.2.0
- **Quest goals in your zone**: for quests still in progress, the nearest place to do them (the mob to kill, the object to use, the creature that drops the item, the spot to explore), with your progress.
- **Saved quests** (per character): right-click a quest row to keep it at the top in every zone. It drops off when you turn the quest in or abandon it.
- Hover tooltips open beside the window instead of on top of it.
- `/wh perf` shows how much time the addon uses.
- NPC world positions are looked up once instead of on every refresh.

## 0.1.0
- First test release: quests ready to turn in (all zones), plus trainers and recipe vendors worth visiting in your zone, with click-to-waypoint.
